dotloom-mcp gallery

Every committed piece of artwork in this repository, rendered from its .pixel source by the engine and published next to what the judgement layer can say about it. Nothing here is a hand-placed screenshot: each image on this page came out of export_png during this build, and each name, code and region came out of evaluate.

How to read this page

Named defects, never a number

Every problem on this page is a code, the dimension that found it, the region it sits in and the what to do line beneath it. That is the whole form on purpose. A single number handed to a reader becomes the target instead of the artwork: this repository once shipped a tool that returned one, a model was told a lake was clean, and the model sanded the lake into a dark flat rectangle to keep it clean. Names survive being ignored; a number does not.

Not measured is not clean

A dimension can abstain: a full-bleed scene has no subject to read a silhouette from, and a document that declares no outline is choosing a style rather than failing one. Those abstentions are printed in their own Not measured block, never folded into the list of things that were checked. An absent measurement and a good result both arrive as a missing number, and only one of them is a compliment.

The dimensions

silhouette, value, palette, noise, outline, motion — each reports per-dimension findings, and the ones that could not run on a given piece say why. The weighted total exists internally for the delivery gate and is deliberately not on this page.
